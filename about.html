Our Mission

We exist to close the gap between how systems are sold and how they're actually used. Too many clinics and businesses invest in powerful platforms — Epic, Cerner, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics — only to struggle with poor implementation, disjointed workflows, and undertrained teams.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Where is Sorian Systems located? +

Sorian Systems is headquartered in the Greater New Jersey / New York metropolitan area. We serve clients across New Jersey, New York, and Trinidad & Tobago — bringing the same standard of excellence to every engagement regardless of geography.

What EHR systems do you implement? +

We are vendor-agnostic and work with the EHR platform that best fits your clinic. Our consultants have experience with Epic, Cerner (Oracle Health), athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, and other major platforms commonly used by clinics in New Jersey and New York.

What ERP platforms do you support? +

We implement SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle ERP, and other leading enterprise platforms. We evaluate your business requirements first and recommend the best-fit ERP solution — we never push a vendor we don't believe is right for you.

Do you work with small clinics? +

Absolutely. Our EHR implementation service is designed for 1–25 provider clinics. Whether you're a solo practitioner in NJ or a multi-specialty group in New York, we tailor the engagement to your size, budget, and specialty.
